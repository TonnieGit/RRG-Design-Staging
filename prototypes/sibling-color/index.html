Design Rationale — Sibling/Color Template

Decisions

All 13 real MAXTRAX MKII colours are on-page as one large, thumb-friendly swatch grid (40px circles, 44px on mobile) rather than a dropdown — clicking any swatch instantly swaps gallery images, price, stock status, SKU and the Colour spec field, with the selected colour name always shown as text next to the grid (not color-only, for accessibility and at-a-glance confirmation). A small orange dot flags limited-stock colours directly on the swatch itself.

Grounded in

Section 1 table (sibling/color's stated challenge: "visual browsing/selection — swatches need to be fast and satisfying to click through"); Section 0.1.6 (thumb-friendly sizing on mobile, explicitly called out for swatch pickers); product-page-requirements.md Section 1.3/2.11 (this is a deliberate structural departure from the live site, which has no swatch picker at all today — each color is a separate URL).

Spec Section 1 taxonomy table; product-page-requirements.md Sections 1.3 & 2.11.

Trade-off / open item

Titanium Grey is the one colour with no scraped discount ($319 flat, vs. $319→$299 on all 12 others) — shown here as real/unmerged data per spec Section 3 step 4, not silently corrected. Needs a client check: genuine pricing difference, or a missed sale flag on that one listing? One more data-fidelity note: Black's two extra thumbnail images are real scraped URLs (filenamed per-SKU) but show generic lifestyle/action photography of an orange board in the field — the live site appears to reuse the same action shots across colour listings regardless of filename. Worth flagging to the client rather than silently swapping in a "cleaner" placeholder.
