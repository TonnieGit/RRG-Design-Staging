    Design Rationale — Fitment Confidence Command Center

    Decisions

    Fitment status is the first thing under the title in a "decision panel," directly followed by the Build Option variant picker, then price, total-cost note, and Add to Cart — resolving spec Section 4 item 1 (a vehicle-specific kit can carry a config-variant alongside fitment logic, not either/or). The picker reuses config-variant's comparison-card pattern rather than introducing a new UI convention, and it live-updates SKU, price, specifications and the Fitting Instructions link the same way. Once the initial panel scrolls out of view, a condensed persistent bar (fitment + price + Add to Cart) pins to the top of the page for the rest of the scroll. Delivery/Click & Collect is now a full-width tabbed widget (not a small button), and Showroom Finder is a standalone high-contrast CTA block that leads with "on display at N stores near you" rather than a plain link.

    Grounded in

    Section 0.1.5 (fitment beside price/CTA, non-negotiable — now true at every scroll depth, not just above the fold); 0.1.3 (reviews visual weight); 0.1.4 (total-cost/shipping visibility, and the Delivery/Click & Collect widget now actually surfaces Standard vs Express cost and per-store stock rather than hiding it behind a click); 0.1.7 (3-second confidence rule). Showroom Finder's stronger framing is a direct response to stakeholder feedback that it should function as its own trust-building CTA, not a passive utility link.

    Baymard Institute; Spiegel Research Center; Blend Commerce PerTronix case study.

    Trade-off

    The gallery still only gets ~55% of the hero width on desktop — that trade-off stands. The two new full-width sections (Delivery/Click & Collect, Showroom Finder) add meaningful page length before the Fitment Gallery; worth watching whether that pushes the Fitment Gallery too far down for lower-intent visitors, versus the trust/logistics payoff for higher-intent ones. The Flat Pack SKU, price and assembly-time figures in the new Build Option picker are fabricated demo data (no real Flat Pack listing exists for this exact Hilux kit yet) — modelled on the real Assembled/Flat Pack pricing ratio seen on the Pioneer 6 Platform pair (RH62112/RH62112F). Needs a real SKU and price from the client before this leaves prototype stage, same caution as the plank-count and package-item SKU flags elsewhere in this project.
