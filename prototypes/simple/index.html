Design Rationale — Simple Template

Decisions

Deliberately the leanest of the four templates: no fitment messaging, no variant picker, no persistent scroll bar. Reuses the same decision-panel/gallery skeleton and component library established for the vehicle-specific template (trust icons, tabs, Delivery/Click & Collect widget, related-products grid) for visual consistency, but strips out everything that doesn't apply to a single-SKU, universal-fit accessory. Showroom Finder was added 2026-09-10 (client ask: available on all products, not just vehicle-specific kits) — toggleable off per-SKU via the Demo State Panel for products that aren't on display anywhere.

Grounded in

Section 1 table (Simple = "fast, low-friction path to cart... don't over-build the page" — this is the explicit design brief for this template, not just a default); the real scraped stock-scarcity message ("This item is limited — Whilst stocks last") is surfaced directly above Add to Cart, matching Baymard guidance that urgency messaging works best adjacent to the purchase action, not buried in a tab.
